We specialize in high-retention video editing across two primary formats: YouTube longform (talking heads, video podcasts, deep-dive documentaries, and educational content) and vertical shortform (Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and TikToks). We do not believe in rigid, one-size-fits-all packages. On our initial call, we look at your current publishing schedule, average raw footage length, and desired motion graphics complexity. We then build a flat, predictable monthly retainer or per-project quote tailored to your exact monthly output.
Our team works inside industry-standard professional suites: Adobe Premiere Pro for core assembly and narrative pacing, Adobe After Effects for bespoke motion graphics and kinetic typography, DaVinci Resolve for cinematic color grading, and Adobe Audition / iZotope for audio cleanup and sound design.
