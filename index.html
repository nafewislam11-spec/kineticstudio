Transparent Answers

Questions You May Ask

Clear, transparent answers about our video editing services and post-production workflow.

We specialize in high-retention video editing across two primary formats: YouTube longform (talking heads, video podcasts, deep-dive documentaries, and educational content) and vertical shortform (Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and TikToks). Every edit includes pacing optimization, motion graphics, audio mastering, and thumbnail packaging.
We do not believe in rigid, one-size-fits-all packages. On our initial call, we look at your current publishing schedule, average raw footage length, and desired motion graphics complexity. We then build a flat, predictable monthly retainer or per-project quote tailored to your exact monthly output.
Our team works inside industry-standard professional suites: Adobe Premiere Pro for core assembly and narrative pacing, Adobe After Effects for bespoke motion graphics and kinetic typography, DaVinci Resolve for cinematic color grading, and Adobe Audition / iZotope for audio cleanup and sound design.
For vertical shortform batches, our typical turnaround is 48 hours. For YouTube longform videos (10–20 minutes), initial cuts are delivered within 48 to 72 hours. Once we establish your brand style guide and ongoing rhythm, deliveries become completely predictable and synchronized with your upload calendar.
We use Frame.io / async video review tools where you or your team can click and leave timestamped comments and visual draw-overs directly on the video player. We offer fast 24-48 hour revision turnarounds until you are 100% happy with the final cut.
You can upload raw camera footage, audio tracks, and assets directly to Google Drive, Dropbox, Frame.io, or your existing cloud storage. We can also provide a dedicated shared cloud folder structured for effortless weekly file transfers.
